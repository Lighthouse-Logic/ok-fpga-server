## When Do You Need a Commercial License?

You should obtain a Commercial License from **Lighthouse Logic** if you plan to:

- Distribute products, test appliances, FPGA bitstreams, firmware images, or host binaries containing or linking to **`ok-fpga-server`** **without releasing your complete combined source code under GPLv3**.
- Embed or ship hardware/software systems to external customers or contract manufacturers where end-user modification or "Installation Information" disclosure (GPLv3 Section 6) is restricted.
- Require **commercial warranties, indemnification, NDA-backed engineering support, custom FrontPanel / FPGA board ports, or Service Level Agreements (SLAs)**.
